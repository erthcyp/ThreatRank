Stage 2

Attribute Completion

Filling in what NVD does not say

The attack graph needs to know what each weakness gives an attacker: can they read data, change it, or take the service down? That is the CVSS impact (C / I / A). When NVD publishes a vector we use it; when it doesn't, a gradient-boosting classifier predicts it from the finding's text, family, solution type, severity, confidence and port.

Stage 5

Budget-Aware Prioritization

Most risk removed per unit of effort

The risk of a finding is R = L × C; the risk of an action is the sum over the findings it closes. Each action costs its tier: patch 1, configuration change 2, replacement 5. Actions are then taken greedily by risk removed per unit of cost until the budget runs out.
